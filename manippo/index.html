ManiPPO Manifold-constrained latent search for behavior-targeted course generation

Andrew Olvera

York College of Pennsylvania

Paper (coming soon) ← All research

Overview

Using a flow-matching VAE trained over a corpus of synthetically generated, highly diverse drone racing courses, we model the course manifold in latent space. We then learn a compass over that latent space, keyed to the behavior the actor is required to acquire.

Together these allow the latent manifold to be searched and traversed in a manifold-constrained manner, generating synthetic courses that teach the actor specific target behaviors. This work is in progress.
