Research

Learning Dynamics in RLVR Near Capacity Andrew Olvera · Preprint forthcoming, 2026 A controlled study of RLVR on a 0.6B model already close to its ceiling on grade school math. Phase 1 fixes a stable optimization baseline across GRPO, DAPO, and group size; phase 2 holds it fixed and varies only the reward. Rewards that consistently separate completions within a group keep the learning signal alive after binary rewards run dry, and every configuration stalls between 70 and 72%, a limit of the model rather than the objective. Tells Andrew Olvera · Preprint forthcoming, 2026 A MicroRTS world action model that infers the opponent from play history under fog of war. An exact tokenizer and a flow dynamics model give a strong semantic simulator; a causal encoder distills a 16-code opponent belief that improves long-horizon return prediction. PMPO policies trained in imagination beat CoacAI in small held-out panels, but the best one uses no belief, and matched belief-conditioned policies did not outperform it: opponent information that predicts well is not yet used well. Starscream Andrew Olvera · Preprint forthcoming, 2026 A supervised-only base model for agile drone racing, scoped without RL to isolate representation learning. DAgger and DART on an MPCC expert, an auxiliary dynamics head, and a causal transformer that reads the next six gates as ordered route tokens and takes a speed command that can be changed at test time and generalizes beyond its training targets. Flying randomized vehicles it was not told about, it completes 45 to 61% of attempts on unseen layouts and recovers from missed gates. The full method distills this privileged teacher into deployment-observation and vision students that infer the vehicle through an auxiliary plant head; final results with the preprint. ManiPPO Andrew Olvera · Work in progress, 2026 A flow-matching VAE trained over a corpus of synthetically generated, highly diverse drone racing courses models the course manifold in latent space. Learning a compass over that latent space, keyed to the behavior the actor needs to acquire, allows manifold-constrained search and traversal to generate synthetic courses that teach the actor specific target behaviors.
