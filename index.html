Research

An Empirical Analysis of Token-Level Rewards and Advantages Andrew Olvera · Preprint forthcoming, 2026 An ablation study of GRPO against DAPO under varying reward formulations, using a small Qwen3-0.6B model on GSM8K-level mathematics. We find that dense, token-level rewards targeted specifically at reasoning structure stabilize learning and improve the policy’s ability to explore toward new global maxima. FlowDreamer MicroRTS Andrew Olvera · Preprint forthcoming, 2026 A flow-matching dynamics model of the MicroRTS game engine that attains high reconstruction and future-state prediction accuracy, enabling an actor to be trained entirely in imagination with PMPO and to beat CoacAI. A self-supervised clustering objective pretrains an encoder whose latents decode into unsupervised clusters of opponent behavior; supplying these to the critic yields a theory-of-mind-style critic that makes PMPO optimization substantially faster and shifts behavior to be more predictive of the opponent, avoiding failure modes observed in a direct ablation against the standard critic. Starscream Andrew Olvera · Preprint forthcoming, 2026 A hybrid DAgger + DART and dynamics-learning pretraining method for scaling generalization in high-speed agile drone racing with extreme sample efficiency, paired with a causal decoder transformer whose sequence design forces geometric continuous trajectory conditioning. ManiPPO Andrew Olvera · Work in progress, 2026 A flow-matching VAE trained over a corpus of synthetically generated, highly diverse drone racing courses models the course manifold in latent space. Learning a compass over that latent space, keyed to the behavior the actor needs to acquire, allows manifold-constrained search and traversal to generate synthetic courses that teach the actor specific target behaviors.
